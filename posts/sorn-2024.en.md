Today's review is a Special Edition that brings together my two most recent meals at Sorn. The first was in May 2024, after the restaurant's renovation, when whispers were going around that the restaurant wanted three stars. The second was in March 2025, after Chef Ice's dream came true: Sorn was awarded three MICHELIN stars, the first restaurant in Thailand to do so and the first Thai restaurant in the world to receive three stars, the highest honour a restaurant can have.

Sorn is the Southern Thai restaurant of Chef Supaksorn "Ice" Jongsiri, set in a restored two-storey house on Sukhumvit Soi 26. The restaurant received one star in the MICHELIN Guide Thailand in 2019, rose to two stars in 2020, and gained its third star in 2025.

Chef Ice has family roots in Nakhon Si Thammarat and grew up helping his grandmother cook Southern food, before working at Baan Ice, his family's Southern Thai restaurant, and then opening Sorn in June 2018. Almost the entire Sorn team came from Baan Ice. Chef Ice taught himself to cook, and has said in an interview: "Don't lie to yourself. Be honest with yourself and with the ingredients, and do your best."

Every ingredient at Sorn comes directly from the 14 provinces of the South, 13 of which border the sea, so the core of the menu here is seafood.

The first thing I want to say is that Sorn is without doubt the best Thai restaurant in the world. There can hardly be a foodie anywhere who does not know this famous Southern Thai restaurant. Although the price is high, Sorn is the Thai restaurant with the highest standards, and what the team creates is worth every baht and every satang. To me, the ingredients and the cooking here are first rate for Thailand, something we should truly be proud of. Personally I think the food after the renovation closure is more elevated and has far more dimension than before. The meal after the renovation was simply superb, and made an unforgettable impression.

On service, there are still small details that could be better, such as the tone of voice when answering the phone, which sounded unfriendly. Overall the service is not bad, from the front-of-house to being shown the way to the restroom, everything is neat and orderly, but personally I feel it still lacks a little charm. The room smelled of disinfectant, and I faintly sensed a damp smell. The restaurant displays ingredients without saying what they are, which may leave foreign guests puzzled.

The most important thing is that, on this return visit, I felt the heat had been toned down. The menu plays more with the melody of flavours and the gradation of taste, with palate-breaking dishes and dishes that soothe the heat, you could say it has more cat-and-mouse play and balance. At that time I believed Sorn would eventually win three Michelin stars, and Chef Ice and the Sorn team have done it.

Let's see what was on the table.

🍽️ The art of taste in this meal:

## The art of taste in this meal

### Hua Sai Fragrant Rice with Giant River Prawn Fat and Samklao (ข้าวหอมหัวไทร มันกุ้งแม่น้ำสามเกลอ)
image: ./images/sorn-2024/01.jpg | A round puffed rice cracker sprinkled with orange prawn fat and coriander, on a grey stone plate | focus=.5,.4
The meal opens with the greeting "Kin khao kan, kin khao reu yang" (let's eat, have you eaten yet?). Fragrant rice from Hua Sai, Nakhon Si Thammarat, comes with giant river prawn fat and samklao, a coriander dipping sauce with long red chilli and vinegar, and fried sago. Samklao is a basic building block of the Thai kitchen, garlic, coriander root and pepper pounded together. The taste has real dimension: hot pepper, sourness from lime, the fragrance of coriander, and slightly crisp fried sago.

### Phuket Abalone Yum (ยำหอยเป๋าฮื้อภูเก็ต)
image: ./images/sorn-2024/02.jpg | Abalone yum, slices of meat stacked in clear liquid in a glass bowl | focus=.5,.5
Phuket abalone dressed as a yum with peeled mangosteen, mamuang bao juice, salak from Trang and ginger. Mamuang bao is a small, sour mango with crisp flesh, from Songkhla. The abalone has a good, chewy texture, and the mamuang bao juice has a light sweet-sour scent that is refreshing.

### Fried Turmeric-Marinated Sea Bream (ปลาทรายหมักขมิ้นทอด)
image: ./images/sorn-2024/03.jpg | Coiled fried turmeric-marinated sea bream with fried leaves behind it, on grey stone | focus=.5,.65
Fried turmeric-marinated sea bream with a scallop sauce, served with lep khrut leaves fried in curry paste. The fish is crisp. I think the scallop flavour stands out more than the fish. A beautiful dish, and Thai in flavour.

### Surat Thani Blue Swimmer Crab Cured in Fish Sauce and Phuket Rum (ปูม้าสุราษฎร์ดองน้ำปลาและรัมภูเก็ต)
image: ./images/sorn-2024/04.jpg | Blue swimmer crab claws, lobster and sea snails arranged on ceramic rocks of various sizes on a wooden table | focus=.5,.5
Blue swimmer crab from Surat Thani, cured in fish sauce and Phuket rum, served with a som jeed granita. Granita is Italian-style coarse shaved ice. In the mouthful there is a little ice, the good umami of the fish sauce, then a light citrus note from the som jeed. Very good flavour, and the crab itself is very good.

### Phuket Seven-Colour Spiny Lobster (กุ้งมังกรเจ็ดสีภูเก็ต)
Seven-colour spiny lobster from Phuket, served with mamuang bao, watermelon sauce and coriander sauce. The lobster meat is bouncy. Personally I feel the prawn flavour does not come through much, but the texture is very good.

### Krabi Sea Snail (หอยหวานกระบี่)
Sea snail from Krabi dressed Thai-style with chilli oil and tao jiao. Tao jiao is fermented salted soybean, deeply savoury, and it is the spiciest of all.

### Squid Stuffed with Lobster (ปลาหมึกยัดไส้กุ้งมังกร)
image: ./images/sorn-2024/07.jpg | Stuffed squid glazed with black ink sauce, sprinkled with red seeds, on grey stone | focus=.5,.5
Squid stuffed with lobster, served with grilled aubergine samklao, Palian pepper and a black sauce made from squid ink. Palian pepper is pepper from Palian district in Trang province. The squid is tender, and the pepper is rather citrusy and hot.

### Radish and Ginger Pickled in Wild Honey (หัวไชเท้าและขิงดองน้ำผึ้งป่า)
image: ./images/sorn-2024/08.jpg | A whole radish with its leaves and ginger pickled in wild honey, in a rough-textured white bowl | focus=.5,.5
The scent of pickled radish, a gentle ginger and the sweetness of honey.

### Kanchupiang (กรรชูเปียง) 🕶️
image: ./images/sorn-2024/09.jpg | Crab claw meat standing in a granite mortar, orange crab roe curry paste beneath | focus=.5,.55
Crab roe "yang matum" with yellow chilli and crab claw meat from Surat Thani. This time it was version 2.0: the curry paste is smooth, the crab roe rich, and it is very spicy, fiery and slightly sour. The crab is very big.

### Coconut Milk Soup with Liang Leaves (ต้มกะทิใบเหลียง)
image: ./images/sorn-2024/10.jpg | A coconut-shell bowl with grilled coconut flesh cut into a flower shape, before the liang leaf coconut milk broth is poured in | focus=.5,.5
Coconut milk soup with liang leaves, coconut flesh, grilled coconut milk and crab-shell oil. Liang is a Southern vegetable. It is light, green and sweet, with a slight astringency from the liang leaves, the crab oil binding the flavours together. This dish soothes the heat of the previous one.

### Kor Lae Cockles (หอยแครงกอและ) 🕶️
image: ./images/sorn-2024/11.jpg | Cockles on skewers brushed with curry paste, grilled on a small charcoal grill | focus=.5,.5
Cockles kor lae, with dried abalone grated over the top. Kor lae is a grilling style, brushing with a coconut-milk curry paste in the Malay tradition of the lower South. The texture of the cockles is very good, the sauce sweet, the curry paste good, and the umami from the dried abalone comes in to supply what the cockles lack.

### Khao Yam with Sai Buri Budu (ข้าวยำน้ำบูดูสายบุรี)
image: ./images/sorn-2024/12.jpg | Brightly coloured khao yam with budu arranged in a circle on a blue-and-white patterned plate | focus=.5,.5
Sai Buri budu khao yam is made with turmeric puffed rice, torch ginger flower and pomelo. Budu is a Malay-style fermented fish sauce, for which Sai Buri in Pattani province is famous. The sweetness leads, with a green astringency from the many vegetables woven through, and the rice is light and crisp. This dish acts as the salad course of the menu.

### Songkhla Lake Fragrant Rice (ข้าวหอมทะเลสาบสงขลา)
image: ./images/sorn-2024/13.jpg | Songkhla Lake fragrant rice cooked in an earthenware pot | focus=.5,.55
Queen fragrant rice from Songkhla Lake, cooked with Ranong mineral water until it rises in the pot.

### Accompaniments (เครื่องเคียง)
image: ./images/sorn-2024/14.jpg | Fresh vegetables and accompaniments in a four-well leaf tray beside a bowl of fresh vegetables | focus=.55,.5
Besides vegetables and chilli in fish sauce, there are red quail eggs pickled in chilli fish sauce, dried sour curry paste with sweet shrimp, and pork crackling with wild mafai chilli paste.

### Stir-Fried Catfish with Sator (ปลาดุกผัดเผ็ดกับสะตอ)
image: ./images/sorn-2024/15.jpg | Spicy stir-fried catfish with sator beans, with a bowl of curry in the middle of a turquoise plate | focus=.5,.55
Chewy-skinned catfish stir-fried with chilli and sator beans, eaten with ma-tum khaek shoots. The fish is very good, extremely tender, and the sourness of the ma-tum khaek shoots helps put out the heat.

### Stir-Fried Morning Glory (ผัดผักบุ้ง)
image: ./images/sorn-2024/16.jpg | Strands of green stir-fried morning glory on a cream ceramic plate with a brown rim, sprinkled with crisp prawn and scallop | focus=.5,.5
Chilled morning glory with the restaurant's own house-made oyster sauce, and crisp prawn and scallop. It leans sweet, but the textures, crisp and soft, contrast.

### Phuket Tiger Prawn Omelette (ไข่เจียวกุ้งลายเสือภูเก็ต)
image: ./images/sorn-2024/17.jpg | A tall omelette topped with a large tiger prawn, on a pale blue plate | focus=.45,.5
A condo-shaped omelette with a big piece of prawn, the palate-breaking dish of the spread.

### Pickled Plum Soup (ซุปต้มบ๊วย)
image: ./images/sorn-2024/18.jpg | Cloudy pickled plum soup in a flower-shaped ceramic bowl | focus=.45,.5
Soup of plums pickled in-house, with a stock simmered from stingray bones and skin, served with stingray from Phuket. Lightly flavoured, it is the final break of the spread. I would call this set the ultimate of the spread-style meal.

### Phatthalung Roti with Beef and Winter Melon Curry (โรตีพัทลุงกับแกงเนื้อใส่ฟัก)
image: ./images/sorn-2024/19.jpg | Square pieces of beef on a roti with yellow curry in a granite bowl | focus=.5,.55
Roti from Phatthalung with a beef and winter melon curry. The curry paste is middling, not very intense, the taste not strong, and the roundness of flavour not very distinct, but the texture of the roti is good, and the beef is good.

### Black Sticky Rice (ข้าวเหนียวดำ)
image: ./images/sorn-2024/20.jpg | Black sticky rice with an orange rolled sweet and a wafer, on a white plate | focus=.55,.5
Black sticky rice with jasmine coconut ice cream and fried nuts. Eaten all together, rich and savoury.

### Loi Kaeo Rambutan with Nam Dok Mai Mango Ice Cream (เงาะลอยแก้วกับไอศกรีมมะม่วงน้ำดอกไม้)
It tastes like loi kaeo.

### Songkhla Egg Cakes (ขนมไข่สงขลา)
It closes with egg cakes from Songkhla.

🕰️ Looking back at the first meal in 2020, in the two-star days

Back in September 2020, Sorn was the last Michelin-starred restaurant in Thailand I had not yet visited, until it changed to an online booking system. At that time the restaurant had just risen to two stars. I said then that Sorn was one of the best restaurants in the country, with an Amuse Bouche among the best in the country, though some main dishes repeated their flavours and the desserts lacked wow. My favourite dishes of that meal were the sour curry of sea bream and the signature kanchupiang.

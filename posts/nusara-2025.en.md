We are used to 4 Hands or 6 Hands collaboration events, but this time the world-famous fine dining Thai restaurant "Nusara" raised the occasion a further notch with a 10 Hands event, hosted by Chef Ton Thitid Tassanakajohn and sommelier Khun Tam Chaisiri.

"Nusara", a restaurant with a wealth of acclaim, including Tatler Best of Asia, the Michelin Guide and Asia's 50 Best Restaurants, opened its doors to Chef Tanaka of the celebrated AT in Paris and Chef Dimi of ORE, which had only just won the MICHELIN Guide Opening of the Year 2025 award.

Chef Atsushi Tanaka, who holds one Michelin star, has gathered invaluable experience working with legendary chefs in Europe, including Pierre Gagnaire, Quique Dacosta and Sergio Herman, as well as famous Nordic restaurants such as Geranium, Frantzén and Oaxen Krog. This has shaped a distinctive culinary identity that blends Nordic and French influences with Japanese foundations in perfect harmony.

Chef Dimitrios Moudios, the Greek chef who caused a stir in the food world with the MICHELIN Guide Opening of the Year 2025 award for ORE, is no less accomplished, with experience at leading restaurants around the world such as Alchemist and Ernst in Berlin, as well as a long stage at Boragó in Chile. He presents food with a South American flavour, a taste rarely found in the eastern hemisphere.

The gentle sweetness at the end of this special dinner was created by Chef Dech Kiukocha of KYO Bar and Gaggan at LV, a pastry chef to watch in this era, winner of Tatler Thailand Best Pastry Chef 2025 and La Liste's Pastry Chef Talents of the Year 2023.

And of course, not to be missed is Chef Ton Thitid Tassanakajohn, the Thai celebrity chef famous around the world, owner of a food empire with many successes, including Le Du, the Thai restaurant with two Michelin stars; Nusara, the ancient-style Thai restaurant inspired by his grandmother; and Samut, the superb seafood restaurant in Phuket; as well as the 2 Knife award from Best Chef Award 2024.

This exciting dinner began at Nuss Bar, Nusara's chic bar, which was named one of Tatler Best Bars in Thailand 2025. We were welcomed with a refreshing aperitif made from calamansi and neem blossom honey, served in a beautifully coloured porcelain cup.

Then we were invited into the bustling kitchen, where we were warmly welcomed with impressive Amuse Bouche from all three chefs: Shima Aji and fresh vegetables wrapped in seaweed from Chef Tanaka; Porcutto, beef wrapped around pickled tomato with caviar, from Chef Dimi; the fragrant grilled pork with betel leaf from Chef Ton; and a golden cup filled with pineapple ice cream, curry powder and crab, a novel yet harmonious flavour, from Chef Dech.

Before going up to the top floor for an Aperitif of Granmonte Shiraz Sparkling Wine, a sparkling red wine that is extremely hard to find, which the restaurant's sommelier whispered to us was a Special Private Label available only at Nusara.

## The art of taste in this meal

### Appetizer
image: ./images/nusara-2025/01.jpg | An appetiser on frosted glass and a shell, with a small cup of clear soup on the table, Nusara, Bangkok
A range of flavours from Nusara - Surf Clam | Lotus | River Fish, with the parts of the lotus carefully prepared: lotus shoots stir-fried in rich shrimp paste with sweet surf clam, crisp-fried soft-fleshed fish under a rounded satay sauce, and a clear soup of lotus petal tea seasoned a little like tom khlong.

### Tuna | Tomato water | Strawberry | Rhubarb
image: ./images/nusara-2025/02.jpg | Sliced tuna scattered with pink petals and yellow oil on a red terracotta plate, Nusara, Bangkok
The next dish is an interesting blend of flavours. Chef Tanaka of AT takes fine Japanese tuna and seasons it in the Nordic style, with yuzu compote, watermelon water and pickled rhubarb, sweet-and-sour and refreshing, before topping it with marigold oil. It is a dish with an interesting Nordic air and the chef's Japanese roots.

### Beans | Naem | Squid | Caviar
image: ./images/nusara-2025/03.jpg | Small green beans in a round dark brown stone pot, Nusara, Bangkok
ORE presents the restaurant's signature **Beans | Naem | Squid | Caviar**, seasoned with naem liquor and sharply sour finger lime, served with snow peas, squid and caviar.

### Crab Curry
image: ./images/nusara-2025/04.jpg | Orange turmeric crab curry in a dark blue-and-white bowl, with crispy fried kung ten in a cup beside it, Nusara, Bangkok
Back to Nusara again with the **Crab Curry**, a punchy turmeric crab curry with coconut milk and horseshoe crab eggs, with the distinctive aroma of the curry paste, eaten with garuda leaves tossed in curry paste and crispy fried kung ten. It is an intense yet unbelievably harmonious pairing with a Marsanne wine from the Rhône in southern France.

### Blue Mussel | Peas
image: ./images/nusara-2025/05.jpg | Fresh green peas in a cream ceramic bowl, Nusara, Bangkok
AT serves the freshness of the sea with **Blue Mussel | Peas**, tender mussels and sweet, juicy peas, dressed with mussel oil and marjoram oil.

### Sweet Potato | Venus Clams | Fingerroot
image: ./images/nusara-2025/06.jpg | A dish under a creamy sauce in a cream-white clay cup on the table, Nusara, Bangkok
From ORE, another dish that surprises, with an interesting pairing of sweet potato, fresh Venus clams and a creamy, distinctive sauce made from fingerroot.

### Razor Clam | Asparagus | Elderflower | Pine
image: ./images/nusara-2025/07.jpg | Soft foamy cream soup with white shellfish in a black bowl, Nusara, Bangkok
The dish that impressed and was delicious beyond forgetting was **Razor Clam | Asparagus | Elderflower | Pine** from AT. The chewy razor clam goes well with sweet, juicy asparagus, served with a softly fragrant elderflower cream and pine oil.

### Shine Muscat | Sea Grape | Rosemary
image: ./images/nusara-2025/08.jpg | A white palate-cleansing dessert scattered with green herbs in a frosted glass bowl, Nusara, Bangkok
The palate cleanser from KYO Bar, **Shine Muscat | Sea Grape | Rosemary**, presents a variety of textures from aloe vera, sweet, juicy Shine Muscat grapes and sour-salty umeboshi, finishing with the complex, cool, refreshing fragrance of rosemary and flowers.

### Duck | Strawberry | Black Garlic | Cherry Blossom
image: ./images/nusara-2025/09.jpg | Sliced roast duck on green leaves with a dark sauce on a cream plate, Nusara, Bangkok
For the main course, ORE presents **Duck | Strawberry | Black Garlic | Cherry Blossom**: roast duck breast cooked just right, served with a sweet-and-sour sauce of strawberry and black garlic, and also a tsukune wrapped in grilled cherry blossom leaf, smoky and fragrant.

### Riceberry | Longan | Black Truffle
image: ./images/nusara-2025/10.jpg | Dessert in a glass cup topped with black truffle and white ice, Nusara, Bangkok
And closing this memorable evening is another dessert from KYO Bar, **Riceberry | Longan | Black Truffle**, a sugar-free khao niao piak longan made from rice milk cream, sweet, juicy longan, crisp rice crackers, and finished with fragrant black truffle. It is a conclusion to a meal truly full of creativity and impressive flavour.

Whatever ranking you look at in the world of food, Odette always stands at the very top: three stars from the Michelin Guide, Best of Asia from Tatler, three Knives from the Best Chef Awards, and a regular place on the lists of both Asia's and the World's 50 Best.
This is surely proof of the sky-high standard of one of the finest French dining rooms in the world.

Odette was founded in 2015 by Chef Julien Royer and named after the chef's grandmother, the main inspiration behind his cooking. The restaurant sits inside the National Gallery Singapore, decorated in soft pastel tones that are easy on the eye, warm and comfortable while still luxurious.

Chef Julien Royer was born in Auvergne and fell in love with cooking as a child through his grandmother's teaching, setting his sights on becoming a professional chef from an early age. He trained under many famous chefs, Michel Bras among them, before moving to Asia to take a position at JAAN (two stars) in the Swissôtel The Stamford, and eventually opened Odette.

Odette presents Modern French cuisine built on top-class produce from around the world, with an emphasis on freshness and high quality. Over the course of the meal we taste outstanding ingredients from every corner of the globe: Amadai from Shimane, lamb from Aveyron, crab from Normandy, Gariguette strawberry, Carotte de Sable and Menton lemon, all of them first-rate. What I had today was the Lunch Terre & Mer at 348 SGD ++. The price may look high for a five-course meal, but the ingredients are of a quality that justifies it, and the cooking is as polished as the price suggests.

Another highlight worth mentioning at Odette is the service, which is lively and caring, and for me Odette is a world-class benchmark. Every server has their own character and a Lively Service style, and the team is extremely attentive, assigning servers according to the language the guest speaks. That day the table next to mine spoke Japanese and another table spoke Korean, and Odette's servers could communicate in both. It was perhaps not perfect: there were crumbs left on the table that day, and a server dropped a piece of bread while serving it with tongs, but it was corrected well.

## The art of taste in this meal

### Grignotages
image: ./images/odette/01.jpg | Three bites: an onion foam tart on a white ceramic stand, a shrimp taco, and a cheese doughnut topped with diced ham in a folded paper case, Odette, Singapore
A French-style doughnut with cheese and ham, fragrant, with the saltiness of the ham and cheese. The dough is slightly chewy, and the whole is middling, to be honest.

The shrimp taco with salsa. The shrimp here is very sweet, probably amaebi, seasoned with yuzu kosho, which goes well with the sweet-sour, faintly spicy salsa. Delicious.

The onion espuma tart. The cream foam on top is slightly salty, set against the sweet caramelised onion below, and the pastry is well made, thin and crisp. Another good bite.

### Mushroom soup, chives sabayon and walnuts
image: ./images/odette/02.jpg | Pale yellow mushroom soup in a white ceramic cup beside a wooden dome lid and wooden saucer, Odette, Singapore
The mushroom soup with chives sabayon and walnuts is eaten with mushroom brioche and cep powder brioche. The bread in this bite is superb, with a faint earthy aroma and a light, fluffy crumb, very well made. Eaten plain it is already delicious, but eaten with the soup it pairs with the sabayon cream and the walnuts, which add balance and link the bread to the mushroom soup in an interesting way.

### NORMANDY BROWN CRAB (Avocado | Nashi pear | Coriander)
image: ./images/odette/04.jpg | A dome of crab meat covered in green apple jelly and garnished with herbs, on a clear glass plate, Odette, Singapore
Crab meat from Brittany mixed with coriander and thinly sliced pear, with avocado purée, served with apple sorbet and topped with green apple jelly. The dish is served cold. The acidity does not stand out, and it is well rounded, with a gentle sweetness, a slight pungency of onion, the richness of avocado and a little finger lime. A harmonious dish, yet complex and with real dimension.

### LOIRE VALLEY GREEN ASPARAGUS (Morel | Dulse seaweed | Miso sabayon)
image: ./images/odette/05.jpg | Green asparagus, morel mushrooms and leafy greens with a cream-coloured sabayon sauce on a wide-rimmed white plate, Odette, Singapore
Asparagus cooked with seaweed, and morels cooked with yellow wine, eaten with the sabayon sauce. The asparagus and morels carry a classic French saltiness which, on its own, I find rather strong compared with the earlier dishes, but once it is cut by the sweetness and the aroma of rosemary in the sauce, it becomes much more interesting.

### SHIMANE AMADAI (Kabu | Yari ika | Pink Garlic velouté)
image: ./images/odette/06.jpg | Grilled amadai with crisp scales on a green sauce, as a cream-coloured velouté is poured, on a large white plate, Odette, Singapore
Amadai from Shimane Prefecture, grilled so that the scales turn crisp, served with a velouté of pink garlic and mussels. The accompaniments are sweet shrimp and squid, steamed Japanese radish, and a watercress foam. I think this dish is almost perfect; technically it is superb. The one snag is that I found the scales still slightly hard and the fish a little fishy. The sauce is well rounded and umami, fragrant with the sea and with a faint, complex note of garlic that stimulates the appetite nicely. What impressed me most were the accompaniments, with their slightly earthy flavour and gentle green aroma, which go very well with the fish.

### AVEYRON LAMB SADDLE (Sand carrot | Menton lemon | Jus tranché)
image: ./images/odette/07.jpg | Two pieces of lamb with crisp skin in a brown jus, with several preparations of carrot and an orange purée, on a white plate, Odette, Singapore
For this dish the chef served the prized Aveyron lamb, roasted with crisp skin. The first thing that made me want to cry out was the texture of the lamb: a thin, crisp skin, and then, a little way in, a layer of fat that melts in the mouth, with no gaminess at all. The flavour is exceptionally clean. The tenderloin is very tender, lightly peppered, and goes with the jus, which is perfectly made.

The sand carrot garnish is cooked in several ways, both as purées and sautéed, its sweetness lifted by just the right amount of Menton lemon.

The lamb fat was turned into a couscous and served with a carrot foam.

### Cleanser

Made from mint, aloe vera and green apple.

### GARIGUETTE STRAWBERRY TART (Crème Fraîche | Hyuganatsu | Basil)
image: ./images/odette/09.jpg | Strawberry tart topped with a round of white cream and Japanese citrus zest on a large white plate, Odette, Singapore
For this dish the chef chose to serve a strawberry tart. A classic is hard to make memorable, but Odette made it beyond my expectations. Every element is well made, from the tart shell to the fresh cream, with strawberries that are sweet, fragrant and tempting, a curd with a gentle creamy sourness and the aroma of the Japanese citrus hyuganatsu, and the cool fragrance of basil. Every element makes this dish balanced and superb.

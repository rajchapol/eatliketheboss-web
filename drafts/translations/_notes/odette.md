# odette - notes for the Boss
Thai source is full of typos and some garbled phrases; my reading is below, please confirm.
- Soup dish: "กินเปล่าๆแช้วไใ่อร่อบ" is unreadable. I translated it as "Eaten plain it is already delicious, but with the soup...". If it meant "not delicious on its own", change that sentence.
- Dish 3 heading: "เนยกะเทียม" read as "เนยกระเทียม" (garlic butter) -> "garlic-herb butter". The dish has no image and no text in the Thai.
- Intro: "อาจจะไม่เพอร์เฟ็กต์ Wine Listในวันนั้นมีเศษขนมปัง..." - the stray words "Wine List" are not translated.
- Crab dish: heading says NORMANDY BROWN CRAB but the text says crab from Brittany (บริทานี่); intro says crab from Normandy. Kept as written.
- Crab dish: "รสหวานแพร" unclear, rendered "a gentle sweetness".
- Lamb dish: "ตัวมันแกะนั้นถูกนำไปทำ couscous ทฝ" - "ทฝ" garbled; rendered "The lamb fat was turned into a couscous". Also "ซี่โครงแกะ" (ribs) vs heading "saddle": rendered just "lamb".
- Amadai: text says "ปลานึ่ง" (steamed fish) though the fish is grilled; rendered "the fish". "หอยแมงภู่" (mussels) in the velouté kept.
- Typos in Thai: Odetteมัก, ไดเริ่ม, คนับ, ซุปเห็ด..ใ่อร่อบ, สตอเบอรรี่, คล่สสิก, มมาก, กะฝระเทีบม, etc.

Smoked Room is the restaurant of Chef Dani García, a celebrated chef from the province of Málaga in Andalusia, set in the Hyatt Regency Hesperia Madrid on the Paseo de la Castellana. It currently holds two Michelin stars in the Michelin Guide España 2026, which it earned after being open for only about six months.

The concept here is Fire Omakase, grilled on a robata with several kinds of wood and charcoal, using smoke gently, like a seasoning. There are currently two omakase menus. The space feels like a secret restaurant, decorated in black, with a Japanese-style counter facing the kitchen, a mirrored ceiling that lets you see the counter and the plating clearly, and just two tables, seating about 14 guests in all.

Chef Dani García first made his name at Calima in Marbella, before opening Dani García Restaurante, which won three Michelin stars in late 2018. Only a few days later, the chef announced he was closing it and switched to opening the steakhouse Leña instead. He is therefore one of the most colourful rebel chefs, and Smoked Room is the restaurant that brings him back into the Michelin Guide. The day-to-day running of the Madrid kitchen belongs to Chef Massimiliano Delle Vedove, an Italian chef whom Dani García chose himself.

Personally, I think this is a restaurant that champions grilling and the aroma of smoke, but does not serve it head-on; instead it blends and presents it in an innovative form. We see the chefs in action in front of us, and there is quite a lot of plating à la miniature, with the chef coming to finish each plate in front of us. The price is fairly reasonable for the ingredients and the number of dishes. Although the smoke aroma feels heavy, the flavours are in fact light and easy to eat.

The food has a light Japanese touch, combined with excellent seafood from the seas of Spain. It is a restaurant that plays a lot with aromatics, even though some dishes are cold dishes whose aromas do not come through clearly. Many dishes have rather new and unusual combinations, but I have to admit that some did not seem to work very well. Together with the fairly heavy smoke aroma in every dish, I personally felt it made the meal somewhat dense, so the course was not very balanced.

There were two dishes this meal for which the Boss had to take his glasses off to taste: the Japanese steamed egg custard with uni and king crab, and the grilled sea cucumber with beef tripe sauce and gochujang.

Let's see what was on the menu.

## The art of taste in this meal

### Brioche and White Truffle Butter
image: ./images/smoked-room/01.jpg | Warm brioche beside white truffle butter on a black plate, Smoked Room, Madrid
It opens with white truffle from Alba, the finest source of truffles, its aroma captured in a wine glass, followed by white truffle butter, a butter made from wild goat's milk, and black pepper, to eat with brioche and bread baked in a wood-fired oven.

### Shrimp from Motril and Noisette Butter
image: ./images/smoked-room/02.jpg | Raw Motril shrimp sprinkled with red flowers on butter sauce in a white ceramic dish, Smoked Room, Madrid
Shrimp from Motril, on the coast of Andalusia, served raw with beurre noisette, or clarified brown butter, and yuzu kosho, the Japanese paste of chilli fermented with salt and yuzu zest. The aroma of butter leads, with a faint yuzu kosho note and gentle saltiness. The shrimp is very sweet and well balanced, not too rich, partly thanks to the bitterness of the flowers, which stops the richness of the butter from lingering in the mouth.

### Smoked Hamachi, Roasted Tomato Essence and Yuzu
image: ./images/smoked-room/03.jpg | Smoked hamachi in a clear golden broth topped with a single green leaf, Smoked Room, Madrid
Hamachi, the yellowtail, cold-smoked and served with a dashi sauce and a clear roasted tomato water, yuzu zest, and oyster leaf, a leaf that tastes like oyster. This dish has a sweet-and-sour umami, a light yuzu aroma, and fish that is not very fatty, with a jelly-like texture.

### Smoked Sturgeon Nitro Tomato, N25 Caviar and Horseradish
image: ./images/smoked-room/04.jpg | A glossy black sphere on white sauce surrounded by caviar and shoots in a ceramic bowl, Smoked Room, Madrid
A signature dish of Chef Dani García, building on the use of liquid nitrogen at -196 degrees Celsius in the kitchen, which the chef pioneered in Spain back at Calima in Marbella. This dish is a nitro tomato with a jelly-like skin and a filling of smoked sturgeon mousse, eaten with finger lime, horseradish and N25 caviar from Yunnan. The tomato has an odd texture, crisp yet not crisp, and I don't know how to describe it. The sauce is very good, finishing with a wine aroma that cuts the cream. The filling smells of fish but is not heavy in flavour. The finger lime and caviar add fun texture and add dimension to the dish in perfect harmony. Another good dish.

### Sweet Corn Chawanmushi, Roasted Leek, King Crab and Smoked Sea Urchin 🕶️
image: ./images/smoked-room/05.jpg | Sweet corn chawanmushi topped with uni and purple flowers, drops of green oil, in a gold cup on a slatted stand, Smoked Room, Madrid
A Japanese steamed egg custard flavoured with sweet corn, topped with smoked uni and king crab from northern Spain, along with roasted leek. Underneath is a dense egg custard, and on top a light, airy egg cream, giving two textures. All the ingredients are good: the crab is sweet, the uni is sweet, and the egg has a gentle salty note of the sea. Done so deliciously.

### Aburi Mackerel and Dashi Tomato
image: ./images/smoked-room/06.jpg | Seared mackerel with green sauce topped with shaved ice in a brown bowl, Smoked Room, Madrid
Aburi mackerel, the fish lightly seared on its surface, with cucumber sauce, coriander oil and pickled cucumber, finished with kakigori, the Japanese shaved ice, made from dried-fish dashi and roasted sweet pepper. The fish has a faint sweet-and-sour taste of pickled mackerel, with the smoky aroma of the searing, and its flesh is rich. The coriander sauce brings a green note that suits seafood, and the pickled cucumber is sour, sweet and cool. The shaved ice, cold yet hot and spicy in flavour, gives an odd sensation that brings out the sweetness of the fish. It is a dish that still puzzles me as I eat it, but is it delicious? Yes. A good dish, in a puzzling way.

### Charbroiled Clams in Tosazu Beurre Blanc and Fresh Wasabi
image: ./images/smoked-room/07.jpg | Grilled clams in a large shell topped with tosazu beurre blanc and fresh wasabi on a dark stand, Smoked Room, Madrid
Torigai from Málaga, lightly grilled over charcoal, served with fresh wasabi and a tosazu beurre blanc, a butter and white wine sauce made with Japanese vinegar blended with bonito dashi. The saltiness of the sauce brings out the sweetness of the shellfish well. The beurre blanc here is creamy, salty-led, not sweet, and sour. The flesh of the shellfish is crisp, while the sweetness comes from the wasabi, which helps balance the dish.

### Grilled Sea Cucumber, Tripe Jus and Gochujang 🕶️
image: ./images/smoked-room/08.jpg | Grilled sea cucumber under a glossy orange sauce with brown crisp pieces and dill shoots on a white plate, Smoked Room, Madrid
Sea cucumber from Galicia, with the skin separated and grilled until crisp and placed on top, while the rest is dried before being cooked in fruit juice and cut into strands. It comes with coriander, a jus reduced from beef tripe, gochujang, the Korean fermented chilli sauce, and angulas, grilled baby eels, a costly luxury ingredient in Spain. The sea cucumber strands are crunchy and crisp, salty with the umami of the sea. The angulas are smoky, crunchy and tender. Good. There is also a smoky heat from the fermented chilli, a light sour-sweetness slipping through, and the green note of coriander, giving a faint hint of taco.

### Tuna Cheek, Pig Tail Jus and Smoked N25 Caviar
image: ./images/smoked-room/09.jpg | Tuna cheek glazed in a dark sauce topped with caviar and nasturtium leaves on a grey stone plate, Smoked Room, Madrid
Tuna cheek glazed with a jus from pig's tail, topped with N25 Oscietra caviar smoked with kombu. The sauce is thick and intense, the collagen setting into jelly. The fish is tender and juicy with a good rich fattiness, matching the faintly smoky aroma of the sauce, and the smoked caviar adds umami.

### Charcoal-Aged Duck with Red Miso and Whiskey
image: ./images/smoked-room/10.jpg | Sliced charcoal-aged duck in a glossy sauce beside a small round item on a lattice-patterned ceramic plate, Smoked Room, Madrid
Challans duck aged over charcoal for two weeks, with inaka red miso, the Japanese country-style miso, a Laphroaig whisky sauce and a pepper compote. The first-rate duck is grilled well, as the restaurant's standard demands. The sauce has the hospital-like aroma that is the signature of Islay whisky, mixed with the blood aroma of the duck and the umami of miso. It is a complex sauce, distinctive and strangely charming, but certainly not a sauce for everyone.

### Charcoal Pumpkin and Mandarin Sorbet
image: ./images/smoked-room/11.jpg | Charcoal-grilled pumpkin with mandarin sorbet in an oval black bowl ringed with pumpkin seeds, Smoked Room, Madrid
Charcoal-grilled pumpkin, pumpkin purée, roasted pumpkin seeds, and mandarin sorbet with pumpkin oil. A dessert that is not sweet: the pumpkin is naturally sweet with nothing added, enriched by the pumpkin seeds, while the sour-sweet sorbet adds interest to the dish.

### Chocolate, Miso and Smoke
image: ./images/smoked-room/12.jpg | Thin chocolate sheets stacked into a mountain shape with white foam on top in a grey ceramic bowl, Smoked Room, Madrid
Chocolate with miso and smoke, with a 10-year-old whisky sauce and a 70% cocoa sponge. Savoury, salty and bitter, with the fruitiness of the chocolate; it is not only sweetness.

### Petit Fours
image: ./images/smoked-room/13.jpg | Petit fours in a stacked drop-shaped wooden box, each compartment holding a different sweet, Smoked Room, Madrid
We finish with small sweets served in a stack of drop-shaped wooden boxes, each compartment holding something different: chocolate, macaron, a golden-brown round sweet, and a red jelly on red flakes.

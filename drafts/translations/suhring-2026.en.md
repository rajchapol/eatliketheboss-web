Back to the same house on Soi Yen Akat once again, after a long absence. This time the restaurant of the Berlin-born twin chefs Thomas and Mathias Sühring is no longer a two-star restaurant: in the MICHELIN Guide Thailand 2026 it was promoted to three stars. It is the second three-star restaurant in Thailand after Sorn, after holding two stars for seven consecutive years, and it is also the first German restaurant in Asia to receive three stars. So the question I carried with me that day was a single one: what has the third star changed?

The first answer was visible even before the first dish arrived. The service team was tighter, and my personal feeling is that the service was noticeably more polished than at two stars. Every server presented the food precisely, and the detail given with each dish felt just right: not so much that it spoiled the fun, and not so little that there was nothing to notice in each plate. Another thing I noticed was that the wine list is more magnificent than before, and the sommelier answered questions and recommended wines superbly. All of the service is professional, worthy of a third star.

The menu I tried was "Erlebnis", the German word for "experience". It still tells the story of homely German food through the childhood memories of the two brothers, from snacks in the style of a Bavarian beer garden and the Rhineland farmers' blood sausage, to grandmother's egg liqueur. For the main course there is a choice between duck and Kagoshima A5 wagyu, which costs extra. This time I chose the duck. On the plate, the flavour profile and the finesse of each dish have been raised considerably from the two-star days. Each dish has elements of aroma and taste with a clear reason for being there, and in every dish the flavours unfold in the mouth in a superb order.

Although the menu may look small, it is very filling, and compared with three-star restaurants abroad, this price is still cheap. This time Sühring is a three-star restaurant I think is worth its price, suited to anyone who wants to try Western food at three-star level and German fine dining at its peak.

## The art of taste in this meal

### Obatzda & Radler
image: ./images/suhring-2026/01.jpg | Pretzel filled with Obatzda cheese beside a Radler beer, Sühring, Bangkok
The meal opened with a Radler, the German beer-and-lemonade mix that cyclists like to drink in the sun. It came with a pretzel filled with Obatzda, a cheese spread blended with butter and paprika that is a staple snack of Bavarian beer gardens, topped with onion. The creamy cheese is soft and tender, laced with paprika, fragrant and faintly sweet. It is a greeting that says from the very first bite that tonight we are in a German home.

### Striped Jack & Horseradish
image: ./images/suhring-2026/02.jpg | Smoked striped jack tart topped with caviar, Sühring, Bangkok
Next came striped jack (shima-aji), kombujime-cured, that is, aged in kombu seaweed so it takes on umami, before being smoked. The fish is mixed with shiso, with chopped horseradish in the centre, served in a tart and topped with Oscietra caviar. This bite has a light smokiness and tender fish, lemon zest keeps it fresh, and the sharpness of the horseradish is gently judged, with the scent of shiso drifting faintly at the tip of the nose before the saltiness of the caviar closes it.

### Brathering & Chervil
image: ./images/suhring-2026/03.jpg | Pickled herring on a black charcoal tart with shiso flowers, Sühring, Bangkok
Then pickled herring, with cucumber gel and mustard, garnished with shiso flowers and dill mayonnaise, served on a black charcoal tart shell. The jelly was much improved, smooth on the way down, with the cool, lightly sweet taste of cucumber, followed by the aroma of pickled fish, salty-sweet, and finished with the fresh green scent of dill. It is a very delicate bite.

### "Himmel und Erde"
image: ./images/suhring-2026/04.jpg | Potato roll filled with blood sausage and green apple, Sühring, Bangkok
"Heaven and Earth". The name comes from a folk dish of Rhineland farmers that pairs apple, standing for heaven, with potato, standing for earth, eaten with blood sausage, or black pudding. Sühring reinterprets it as a potato roll filled with blood sausage and apple chutney, topped with green apple and marjoram mayonnaise. In this bite the marjoram rises first, the outer shell is light and crisp, the apple sweet and sour, and there is sausage to chew in the middle. It is a very complex and beautiful bite.

### Leek & Truffle
image: ./images/suhring-2026/05.jpg | Leek mousse tart with thinly sliced truffle, Sühring, Bangkok
A leek tart with leek and truffle mousse, fried leek and autumn truffle from Burgundy. The leek gives richness and a distinct aroma, with a faint truffle scent leading, a mousse-like texture, then jelly, and a light bitterness from the fried leek follows so that it never feels cloying. The flavour and dimension of this bite are excellent, but personally I thought the tart shell was a little too thick.

### Sweet Shrimp / Tomato / Tarragon 🕶️
image: ./images/suhring-2026/06.jpg | Japanese sweet shrimp with peach and tomato on a tart, Sühring, Bangkok
Japanese sweet shrimp comes with Japanese peach, semi-dried tomato from Chiang Mai and crisp kombu, on a tart shell made from shrimp shells, dressed with a sauce of peach and bisque. At first the green scent of the vegetables leads, mixed with the sea aroma of seaweed, but once it is in the mouth the taste is nothing like I expected. The sauce is luxurious, sweet-sour from the peach layered over the umami of the shrimp, the shell is crisp, the shrimp tender, the peach crunchy, and the flavours reveal themselves slowly in the mouth. The dimension of both flavour and texture in this dish is very good.

### Scallop & King Crab / Turnip / Beechnut 🕶️
image: ./images/suhring-2026/07.jpg | Hokkaido scallop with crab and turnip in an almond milk sauce, Sühring, Bangkok
Next, a kombujime Hokkaido scallop with pickled German turnip, kombu, steamed crab, almond milk and beechnut oil. This dish is very good. The beetroot is crunchy, sweet and sour, the turnip sweet-sour and slightly jelly-like, the almond milk sauce gives a cool fragrance but a light taste, and the oil adds richness. Underneath, a faint citrus scent rises, probably orange marmalade, which adds dimension to the dish superbly. The textures are complete: nutty, the springiness of jelly, crunchy vegetables, tender scallop and bouncy strands of crab meat.

### Rockfish / Mussel / Verbena
image: ./images/suhring-2026/08.jpg | Kinki rockfish in a beurre blanc sauce with caviar, Sühring, Bangkok
Kinki, a rich-fleshed rockfish from the northern Sea of Japan, dressed with a beurre blanc made with mussels and Vin Jaune from the Jura, verbena oil and Kaviari Kristal caviar. The creamy sauce leads with sweetness and then salt, the verbena oil is very fragrant, and the fish is rich and very tender. The saltiness comes from the caviar, which personally I thought was a touch too much, but I doubt anyone would complain.

### Lobster / Summer Squash / Dill
image: ./images/suhring-2026/09.jpg | Grilled lobster with squash and bisque sauce, Sühring, Bangkok
Grilled lobster with butternut squash stuffed with claw meat, dressed with bisque and dill oil, alongside grilled courgette, croutons and parsley mayonnaise. The lobster meat is firm and bouncy with a pronounced smokiness, and the creamy bisque sauce leans sweet. It is a dish executed to the restaurant's standard.

### Duck / Plum / Cru de Cacao
image: ./images/suhring-2026/10.jpg | Crisp-skinned duck breast with plum purée, Sühring, Bangkok
My main course was the duck. It starts with the breast, seasoned with strong pepper and sea salt, well cooked, the skin crisp and the meat not tough at all, with a firm, springy bite throughout. It is eaten with a plum and cacao purée, sweet-sour, which acts as a brake on the richness, with cacao nibs as the link. The duck leg comes with turnip and truffle, rich and creamy, and it closes with a slightly bitter coffee sauce that lends umami in perfect balance.

### Sorrel / Apple / Buttermilk
image: ./images/suhring-2026/11.jpg | Dessert of sorrel leaves with apple and buttermilk, Sühring, Bangkok
The first dessert uses sorrel leaves, apple juice, buttermilk jelly and white meringue. The texture is soft, crisp and light, with a sweet-sour taste and a slight astringency from the sorrel.

### "Rote Grütze"
image: ./images/suhring-2026/12.jpg | Rote Grütze red berry pudding with vanilla cream, Sühring, Bangkok
Rote Grütze is a red berry pudding from northern Germany. Here it is served with vanilla cream, chocolate cake, mixed berries, berry jam and berry sorbet.

### Oma Christa's Eierlikör & Feines Gebäck
image: ./images/suhring-2026/13.jpg | Eierlikör egg liqueur with small pastries, Sühring, Bangkok
It closes with Eierlikör, egg liqueur from Grandmother Christa's recipe, served with small pastries, or Feines Gebäck: a way of sending guests home with the taste of a family kitchen.

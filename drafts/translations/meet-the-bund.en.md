This one came recommended by my lovely "big sister" Pauline, who told me I simply had to go. She was right, and I was not disappointed.

The moment you step across the threshold of Meet The Bund at Skyline, a calm, hushed, spotless space up at the level of the skyline wraps around you. It feels like slipping into another dimension, cut off from the bustle of Shanghai, with a warm white mist drifting through the room.

Meet The Bund stands for the philosophy of "Modern Min Cai", lifting traditional Fujian (Hokkien) cooking with a forward-looking vision. Nothing is seasoned so hard that it hides the true taste; instead, the finest produce from the sea and the mountains of Fujian is left to shine on its own. Fujian cooking is said to be "eight parts mountain, one part water, one part field" (八山一水一分田), so this kitchen brings together fresh seafood from the coast, wild ingredients from the hills and fine tea. If Cantonese cooking is the art of freshness and Sichuan is all about fire, Fujian is umami that runs deep and gentle.

The kitchen is led by Chef Chen Zhiping (陈志平), a picture of the new generation of chefs who truly understand their roots. Born in Fujian, he grew up and trained in Quanzhou before stepping into the Shanghai arena. His gift for linking past and future earned him the Michelin Young Chef Award 2021 and the Black Pearl young chef award 2026.

The food here captures modern Min Cai beautifully: light flavours that lift the best seasonal produce. Many dishes draw their depth from the broth, from Fujian's famous Buddha Jumps Over the Wall to the vegetables, the noodles and the taro, true to the saying "one pot of broth, ten transformations" (一汤十变). Chef Chen builds on long, patient double-boiled broths to coax out every bit of umami, and braises ingredients whole to let that umami ring out clearly.

Fujian is, of course, the home of many of the world's finest teas, so I recommend the tea pairing. Its light character suits the style of the food, and every cup is brewed fresh, one glass at a time, never left steeping. It may look expensive, but sipping hot tea made cup by cup is something that soothes the mind and lifts the mood.

Meet The Bund at Skyline is first-class proof that Min Cai can be raised to remarkable refinement and complexity. The cooking stands out for flavours that are gentle and simple, yet carry a deep, profound umami. It is a fine example of the style.

The food is delicious and the menu easy to enjoy (with the one exception of the sea worm jelly, which may surprise a few). Add the million-dollar panorama over Shanghai, and this is a food lover's heaven worth experiencing for yourself.

## The art of taste in this meal

### 马东蜜兰香单丛 (Wudong Honey Orchid Dan Cong)
The meal opens by waking the senses with a tea from the peaks of Wudong. Light on the palate, it frames the style of Fujian cooking perfectly.

### 五香乾坤包 (Fujian Five-Spice Rolls)
image: ./images/meet-the-bund/01.jpg | Two golden fried tofu-skin rolls topped with a crisp wafer and red sauce on a white plate, Meet The Bund at Skyline, Shanghai
Tofu skin wrapped around a shrimp filling with just the right chew, springy but not overly bouncy, studded with the crunch of water chestnut and mushroom, and a soft scent of Chinese celery. Light and faintly sweet, never heavy, with a gentle waft of five-spice.

### 樱花虾头水紫菜 (Fried Seaweed and Sakura Shrimp Head)
image: ./images/meet-the-bund/02.jpg | Two round balls of black crisp-fried seaweed on a white plate, Meet The Bund at Skyline, Shanghai
The sea comes first, with a light sourness, followed by the umami of early-season head-water laver, fried crisp and served whole. Picking it up, you feel a soft, mochi-like chew, matched perfectly by the crunch of sakura shrimp powder scattered through it.

### 厦门土笋冻 (Xiamen Sipunculid Worm Jelly)
image: ./images/meet-the-bund/03.jpg | Clear jelly cut into squares with sea worms inside, topped with green herbs, on a white plate, Meet The Bund at Skyline, Shanghai
A Xiamen street classic that may look alarming, but tastes nothing like you fear! The wobbly jelly leads with a gentle heat and a light sourness, while the chewy crunch of the sea worms inside makes every bite good fun.

### 漳州嫩姜捞拌螺片 (Zhangzhou Tender Ginger and Hetian Chicken with Sliced Snail)
image: ./images/meet-the-bund/04.jpg | Sliced conch and chicken on crisp fried taro strands in a clear scalloped glass bowl, Meet The Bund at Skyline, Shanghai
At the base, crisp fried taro strands add richness. Then comes cold-dressed Hetian chicken, firm in the flesh with a pleasingly chewy skin, tossed with sliced conch that is crunchy and not the least bit tough. A sauce led by sweet-sour vinegar, with soy umami and the aroma of fried shallots, brings it all together.

### 佛跳墙 (Buddha Jumps Over the Wall) 🕶️
image: ./images/meet-the-bund/05.jpg | Sea cucumber on a porcelain spoon lifted from a white tureen of Buddha Jumps Over the Wall in a dark brown broth, Meet The Bund at Skyline, Shanghai
The ultimate Hokkien signature. The broth is rich and round, wonderfully sweet and generously loaded. Chinese wine leads the way with real depth, followed by the gentle sweetness of the fish. It is packed with collagen, yet never feels heavy to sip. The abalone was only middling, but the very generous portion of fish maw makes this a dish that is outrageously good value!

### Razor Clam and Bamboo Pith Soup
image: ./images/meet-the-bund/06.jpg | Clear pale-yellow soup with white razor clam meat in a white porcelain cup, Meet The Bund at Skyline, Shanghai
A crystal-clear broth with nothing to distract, yet a deep, rounded flavour beneath: all clarity and umami. The fresh razor clams are bouncy and sweet, leaving a beautiful aftertaste that lingers in the throat.

### 大红袍花椒重壳蟹 (Double-Shelled Crab Baked with Da Hong Pao Peppercorn)
image: ./images/meet-the-bund/07.jpg | A whole orange crab on tea leaves and dark red roasted peppercorns in a white glazed clay pot, Meet The Bund at Skyline, Shanghai
Excellent! The crab is baked with the smoke of Da Hong Pao tea leaves. The chef uses a crab in the middle of moulting, with a soft new shell and firm meat, so you get two textures in a single bite. A faint thread of mala heat brings out the sweetness of the crab even more. The pairing, a pine-smoked Lapsang Souchong, has a deep smokiness that suits this crab beautifully.

### Steamed Rice Cake with Mud Crab
image: ./images/meet-the-bund/08.jpg | Steamed rice topped with white crab meat and orange crab roe in a red lacquered wooden cup, Meet The Bund at Skyline, Shanghai
Steamed rice with a soft, sticky chew, almost like gently cooked glutinous rice, rounded with a whisper of soy and topped with carefully picked crab meat and roe.

### 原创葱头油肉汁焗荔浦芋头 (Red Shallot Infused Oil and Pork Gravy Baked LiPu Taro)
image: ./images/meet-the-bund/09.jpg | A cylinder of LiPu taro in a glossy brown braising sauce, topped with fried shallots and spring onion, on a white plate, Meet The Bund at Skyline, Shanghai
Dense LiPu taro that melts lightly on the tongue, baked in a rich, sweet-leaning red-braise sauce, with a sweetness from the fried shallots at the tip of the tongue. Simple, yet intensely deep in flavour.

### 炙烤东山巴浪鱼 (Grilled Dong shan mackerel)
image: ./images/meet-the-bund/10.jpg | Grilled mackerel with blistered skin in a reddish-brown soy sauce, topped with sliced spring onion, on a white plate, Meet The Bund at Skyline, Shanghai
Mackerel from Dongshan Island, so tender the fat melts away, grilled until irresistibly fragrant. With a light soy sauce, it simply works, effortlessly.

### 高汤浸时蔬 (Poached Seasonal Vegetables in Broth)
image: ./images/meet-the-bund/11.jpg | Green leafy vegetables and wood ear mushrooms in a white creamy soup in a white flower-shaped bowl, Meet The Bund at Skyline, Shanghai
A palate refresher: a gentle, creamy soup with a light peppery note, full of sweet, crisp greens, tender shoots, golden mushrooms and wood ear.

### 招牌闽南卤面 (Signature southern fujian lor mee)
image: ./images/meet-the-bund/12.jpg | Noodles in a thick orange broth topped with dried shrimp and fried shallots in a white bowl, Meet The Bund at Skyline, Shanghai
Southern Hokkien lor mee. Personally, I found the soft, chewy noodles a touch too soft, but the broth is creamy with a light scent of shrimp, never overpowering, and loaded with dried shrimp, greens, fresh prawns and oysters. A warm close to the savoury courses.

### 漳平水仙 (Zhangping Shuixian)
Shuixian tea from Zhangping, served to clear the palate.

### 百合建宁莲子冻 (Lotus seed with fresh lily)
image: ./images/meet-the-bund/13.jpg | Creamy white lotus seed jelly topped with fresh lily petals arranged like a flower in a white porcelain cup, Meet The Bund at Skyline, Shanghai
To finish, a jelly of Jianning lotus seeds (once a tribute ingredient for the imperial court) with fresh lily. Cool, gently sweet, and a clean, beautiful way to end.

In 2020, Sorn was the last Michelin-starred restaurant in Thailand I had not yet been to, because I had long given up on its notorious booking system and resolved not to go until the restaurant changed it. At last Sorn switched to online booking, with no more scrambling to get through on the phone. At that time the restaurant had only just moved from one star to two in the MICHELIN Guide, and had just won the Highest Climber Award at Asia's 50 Best Restaurants 2020, at number 16.

In short, Sorn is without doubt one of the best restaurants in the country for me, and its standards fully match its two-star status. The captivating strength is its use of ingredients from the South throughout, even the water for cooking the rice being Southern mineral water. The service is excellent, thorough without being stifling, and the explanation of each dish flowed smoothly with full detail. Before the meal began, Chef Ice came to the table to ask whether we had any food allergies and how spicy we wanted it, since everyone's tolerance for heat differs. That day I found the heat just right, on the fiery side, and although it was spicy in the full Thai way, alternating spicy and non-spicy dishes made it feel less hot than it was.

Nearly twenty dishes, and everyone at the table was so full we could almost crawl home. Judged by the effort in presentation, the technique and the quality of the ingredients, I think it is very good value. I rank the Amuse Bouche among the best in the country, with flavours stepping through heat and weight, heavy against light, in layers. But several of the main dishes taste so close to one another that they may be a little boring, such as the crab curry roti and the kanchupiang, or the kor lae beef and the beef curry rice, and the desserts, though delicious to standard, lacked the wow that the savoury dishes had. Part of that may have been because I was full.

## The art of taste in this meal

### Khud Khat Khoei (คุดคัดเคย)
image: ./images/sorn-2020/01.jpg | Peeled mangosteen sprinkled with toasted coconut and shrimp paste in a white ceramic bowl with a blue pattern, Sorn
Mangosteen from Khiriwong, Nakhon Si Thammarat, sprinkled with toasted coconut and shrimp paste. "Khat" in Southern dialect means to peel. This may be the most delicious mangosteen I have ever eaten: white flesh, sweet with a touch of sourness, crisp and never mushy, with no astringency at all, and a gentle fragrance of shrimp paste and coconut.

### Cucumber with Shrimp Paste and Tayu Nam Prik (แตงกวากะปิเคย น้ำพริกตาหยู)
image: ./images/sorn-2020/02.jpg | A bowl of cucumber with tayu nam prik topped with cashew nuts, on a wooden board, Sorn
Cucumber eaten with shrimp paste and tayu nam prik, "tayu" being the Southern word for cashew nuts. The taste is rich with a hint of sweetness and salty umami, a faint note of shrimp paste, then a little heat at the tip of the tongue, finishing with the freshness of the cucumber. Simple, yet complex in layers.

### Sea Cicadas from Mai Khao Beach (จักจั่นทะเลหาดไม้ขาว)
image: ./images/sorn-2020/03.jpg | Sea cicadas stir-fried with smoked squid tentacles on a white ceramic plate, Sorn
Sea cicadas from Mai Khao Beach, Phuket, sautéed in rice bran oil, with squid tentacles smoked with lemongrass and feather seaweed powder. Sea cicadas live only on beaches with clean water, so they serve as a gauge of water quality. Lightly salty and very crisp, but the trump card is the squid tentacle, dry and chewy, slightly crisp at the surface and smoky in scent, with a superb texture.

### Seven-Colour Spiny Lobster (กุ้งมังกรเจ็ดสี)
image: ./images/sorn-2020/04.jpg | Lobster topped with herb granita on a blue-patterned plate, Sorn
Phuket spiny lobster cured in fish sauce, with Ko Sukon watermelon and a herbal nam jim granita. The prawn flesh is of very high quality, sweet-sour with a hidden heat that lifts the sweetness of the lobster, closing with the cool sweetness of the watermelon. Beautifully balanced and very delicate.

### Satun Conch Phla (พล่าหอยสังข์สตูล)
image: ./images/sorn-2020/05.jpg | White conch phla in a black earthenware bowl, Sorn
Phla of conch from Satun with grilled chilli oil. The conch is cooked to perfection, crunchy and not tough, with a faint aroma of chilli oil and a touch of heat that lifts the sweetness of the shellfish.

### Tapi River Prawn with Chaiya Salted Egg (กุ้งแม่น้ำตาปีกับไข่เค็มไชยา)
image: ./images/sorn-2020/06.jpg | Fried river prawn head topped with yellow Chaiya salted egg and a grilled prawn tail on a wooden board, Sorn
A Surat Thani dish, served two ways. A fried prawn head stuffed with steamed prawn fat and topped with Chaiya salted egg: gentle, creamy, umami, with the scent of salted egg, prawn fat and charcoal all present. The head is crisp but not hard, excellent. The tail is grilled over a gentle fire and served with bird's-eye chilli nam prik and ngoh rongrian rambutan; the flesh is crisp and bouncy, but I think it is a little too sweet and overwhelms the nam prik.

### Kanchupiang (กรรชูเปียง) 🕶️
image: ./images/sorn-2020/07.jpg | Grilled crab claw meat standing in a granite mortar with yellow nam prik, Sorn
The restaurant's signature: large pieces of Surat crab claw meat, steamed and grilled, with yellow nam prik pounded fresh every day and "matum" crab roe. In the mouth the flavour is intense, sweet and sour at the front, then vanishing quickly, followed by the sweetness of the crab meat and creamy crab fat, before closing with the heat of the yellow chilli. With just a little smokiness it would be perfect.

### The Ultimate Coconut (สุดยอดมะพร้าว)
image: ./images/sorn-2020/08.jpg | White coconut milk soup in a coconut shell, Sorn
A palate-breaking dish after the heat: a warm soup of freshly squeezed coconut milk, like tom kha but with the scent of the sea instead of herbs. Salty from dried seafood and grilled shrimp paste, sweet from liang leaves, topped with dried scallop oil, slightly spicy, gently sweet, very umami, and it brings to mind the southern sea.

### Kor Lae Beef (เนื้อกอและ)
image: ./images/sorn-2020/09.jpg | Skewers of grilled kor lae beef on a small charcoal grill beside chopsticks, Sorn
Made from retired dairy cows aged seven to eight years, so that a life is not wasted. The sauce is sweet from nam dok mai mango, with oyster mushrooms and pickled shallots wrapped in grilled betel leaves. The beef is not as tough as you would think: rich, sweet and fatty, fun to chew, fragrant with curry paste, with the mushrooms and shallots cutting the flavour nicely.

### Khao Yam (ข้าวยำ)
image: ./images/sorn-2020/10.jpg | Khao yam arranged in green, yellow and pink mounds on a large plate, Sorn
Thapthim Siam pomelo, budu, torch ginger flower, bean sprouts, Songkhla mamuang bao and rice crisped by three days in the sun. Light in flavour, with the budu giving a touch of marine umami that does not smother the ingredients. Chewing brings something different each time, crunchy here and crisp there, closing with the scent of rice at the tip of the nose. My companions thought it ordinary, but I think it is a very good opening dish for the spread.

### Rice Accompaniments (เครื่องเคียงข้าว)
image: ./images/sorn-2020/11.jpg | A spread of nam prik, lon, sweet pork and vegetables on a leaf plate, Sorn
Coconut-shell nam prik with chee nok guava: coconutty in aroma, sweet and creamy, hot at the tip of the tongue, and then the guava cuts in until the heat is just right. Sea urchin lon with white turmeric: the rich, sweet, fatty lon cut by the clean, sharp white turmeric, a perfect match. And the sweet pork, which I have never met this delicious anywhere.

### Stir-Fried Roe Squid with Shrimp Paste and Sator (หมึกไข่ผัดกะปิสะตอข้าว)
image: ./images/sorn-2020/12.jpg | Roe squid stir-fried with shrimp paste and sator beans on a blue plate, Sorn
Crisp and fragrantly charred at the surface, with chewy, crisp flesh and slightly tough roe inside. The house-made shrimp paste, sweet, salty and umami, cuts the sharpness of sator beans that are not bitter at all. Delicious, and it recalls the South.

### Wild Vegetable Bone Soup (ซุปกระดูกผักป่า)
image: ./images/sorn-2020/13.jpg | Clear soup with wild vegetables in a white flower-shaped bowl, Sorn
A bone stock enriched with dried shrimp or dried scallop and seasoned with light soy sauce. The wild vegetables smell slightly bitter, but sipping it gives a gentle sweetness from the bones, a hint of sea salt, and a light kick of pepper to finish. An ordinary soup whose flavours arrive in superb order.

### Sea Bream Sour Curry (แกงส้มปลาทราย) 🕶️
image: ./images/sorn-2020/14.jpg | Orange sour curry with whole fried sea bream with its scales and fish roe in a ceramic bowl, Sorn
My favourite dish of the spread. Krabi red sea bream, deep-fried with its scales and basted with oil, with Ryukyu fish roe, "matum" and vegetables. The curry is a reduced sour curry: refreshingly sour at the front, low in sweetness, followed by heat at the tip of the tongue. The Ryukyu fish roe, which is often hard and gritty, burst perfectly in the mouth. The scaly fish is crisp, with soft, fluffy flesh no worse than a famous kaiseki restaurant in Japan. It is my ideal sour curry.

### Stir-Fried Lobster with Pumpkin and Fried Egg (ผัดกุ้งมังกรฟักทองไข่ดาว)
image: ./images/sorn-2020/15.jpg | Stir-fried lobster topped with a fried egg served in a pumpkin, Sorn
The lobster was very crisp and bouncy, and the sweetness of the pumpkin cut the fried egg's aroma with unbelievable harmony. Eaten with the sour curry, it matches even better.

### Charcoal-Grilled Crab Curry with Roti (ปูย่างถ่านราดแกงกับโรตี)
image: ./images/sorn-2020/16.jpg | Charcoal-grilled crab meat shaped into a ball in orange curry in a blue bowl, Sorn
Charcoal-grilled crab meat and crab roe, very intense in flavour, rich, sweet and slightly salty, followed by fierce heat. I think the curry overwhelms the sweetness of the crab and the aroma of the roe, so it is not as balanced as it should be. Sorn can do better than this.

### Rice Cooked in Coconut Milk with Beef Curry (ข้าวมันแกงเนื้อ)
image: ./images/sorn-2020/17.jpg | Coconut rice with beef curry, beef crackling and pickled papaya on a banana leaf, Sorn
Alongside pickled papaya and beef crackling. The coconut rice is fragrant and not soggy. The curry uses old dairy cows like the kor lae dish, so the flavours are so close that it feels boring, and eaten with the coconut rice it is slightly cloying. But what impressed me was the beef crackling, crisp and fragrant with the smell of beef, rich and a little salty, and delicious just mixed with fish sauce and eaten with rice.

### Torch Ginger Flower Sorbet with Jelly (เชอร์เบทดอกดาหลากับเยลลี่)
image: ./images/sorn-2020/18.jpg | Pink torch ginger flower sorbet with jelly in a round stone bowl, Sorn
A palate cleanser, lightly sweet-sour with the distinctive scent of torch ginger. The jelly is not sticky or hard to swallow.

### Soy Milk Ice Cream (ไอศกรีมน้ำเต้าหู้)
image: ./images/sorn-2020/19.jpg | Soy milk ice cream with syrup, pa thong go and black sesame mousse in a green bowl, Sorn
Dressed with syrup from Ko Libong, with black sesame mousse and pickled ginger (the alternative was longan sago pearls in syrup). It is the most ordinary dish of the meal. The ice cream is rich, smoky and faintly of soybean, but the texture is not yet smooth enough for a two-star restaurant. The black sesame cream and the sweet-hot pickled ginger go well together. What impressed me instead was the pa thong go, chewy and fluffy, crisp and hot, and very good.

### Betong Shogun Mandarin Loi Kaeo (ส้มโชกุนเบตงลอยแก้ว)
image: ./images/sorn-2020/20.jpg | A canned-fruit-style tin of Betong Shogun mandarin loi kaeo with an orange pattern, Sorn
A seasonal fruit loi kaeo that looks like canned fruit. This time it was Shogun mandarins from Betong in ginger syrup. Not too sweet, it keeps a gentle sweet-sour taste and the aroma of the mandarin well.

### Kopi, Chrysanthemum Tea and Egg Cakes (โกปี๊ ชาดอกเบญจมาศ และขนมไข่)
image: ./images/sorn-2020/21.jpg | Golden baked egg cakes in brass moulds, Sorn
It closes with Ceylon tea or Southern-style kopi. I found the coffee unremarkable, but the chrysanthemum tea, strong, so rich that I asked for refills several times. The egg cakes are made fresh, buttery and rich, and if they stick in your throat you can dip them in the tea.

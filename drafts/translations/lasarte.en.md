Lasarte is the restaurant of Chef Martín Berasategui, a legend of Basque cooking, set in the Monument Hotel on the corner of Carrer de Mallorca, near Passeig de Gràcia in the heart of Barcelona. It currently holds three Michelin stars in the Michelin Guide España 2026, having won the third as far back as the Michelin Guide 2017, and was one of the first restaurants in Barcelona to reach three stars.

The name comes from Lasarte-Oria, the town near San Sebastián that is home to Martín Berasategui's flagship restaurant, which also holds three stars. The Barcelona branch opened in January 2006. The dining room is decorated in golden tones, with an undulating ceiling and lamps inspired by jellyfish.

Chef Martín Berasategui is Basque, born in San Sebastián in 1960. He began working in kitchens at the age of 14 at Bodegón Alejandro, his family's restaurant, where he also earned his first Michelin star, before opening his own restaurant in Lasarte-Oria in 1993. He is now the Spanish chef with the most Michelin stars in total: 11 in the Michelin Guide 2026.

At Lasarte, the head chef is Paolo Casagrande, an Italian from the Veneto region who worked in France with Chef Alain Solivérès before joining Berasategui in 2003, then ran M.B. in Tenerife, where he won his first star in 2009, and has been at Lasarte since 2012, leading it to three stars.

The menu here is the joint work of the two chefs, with both à la carte dishes and tasting menus. There is also Il Milione, a private room for 2 to 8 guests inspired by the travels of Marco Polo. The signature dish that everyone who comes must try is the mille-feuille of smoked eel with foie gras and green apple, which Chef Berasategui created in 1993. I have to say this dish was so good it brought me to tears.

For me, Lasarte is the definition of perfection, a harmonious meeting point of classic French cooking and Spanish cuisine. Both the decor and the tableware are first class. The service is of a very high standard, not only the Restaurant Manager but also the sommelier and every member of staff, who looked after every table superbly and equally. Everyone was extremely professional and courteous.

The dishes have complex and varied components, yet all are combined in harmony and balance. The plating may look simple, but the timing and serving temperature have to be exact. The wine list is spectacular and fairly expensive, but look carefully and there are interesting options that offer good value. I think just coming to taste the signature eel sandwich is worth the trip.

## The art of taste in this meal

### Peas, Chives and Pickled Cucumber
image: ./images/lasarte/01.jpg | Peas with chives and pickled cucumber, Lasarte, Barcelona
Peas with chives and lightly sour pickled cucumber. The pea cream is very sweet, and the balance of flavours is excellent.

### Fish with Fried Potato Dough and Red Cabbage
image: ./images/lasarte/02.jpg | Fish with fried potato dough and red cabbage, Lasarte, Barcelona
Fish with fried potato dough and red cabbage. The fish in this bite is tender, though to me it had a slightly fishy smell.

### Prawn with Cheese and Pine Nuts
image: ./images/lasarte/03.jpg | Prawn with cheese and pine nuts, Lasarte, Barcelona
Prawn with cheese and pine nuts. This bite is sweet and creamy, with a faint aroma of rosemary and a note of orange zest. A good finish.

### Red Prawn Mochi
image: ./images/lasarte/04.jpg | Red prawn mochi, Lasarte, Barcelona
Red prawn mochi with prawn fat, topped with a citrus aroma. Not salty, with an intense flavour that is not as marine as you might expect.

### Financier, Sweet Pepper and Wagyu Fat
image: ./images/lasarte/05.jpg | Financier with sweet pepper and wagyu fat, Lasarte, Barcelona
Financier, sweet pepper and wagyu fat, topped with ham. The sweet, slightly hot pepper contrasts with the gentle saltiness of the ham.

### Bloody Mary Sphere
image: ./images/lasarte/06.jpg | Bloody Mary sphere, Lasarte, Barcelona
A Bloody Mary sphere, the cocktail of tomato juice and vodka. Sweet, sour and spicy.

### Mussel and Tomato Foam
image: ./images/lasarte/07.jpg | Mussel and tomato foam, Lasarte, Barcelona
Mussel with tomato foam. The set ends on a spicy note, the mussel springy and crisp, the foam sour.

TASTING MENU

### Smoked Eel, Foie Gras and Green Apple 🕶️
image: ./images/lasarte/08.jpg | Smoked eel mille-feuille with foie gras and green apple, Lasarte, Barcelona
A mille-feuille of smoked eel layered with foie gras and green apple, caramelised on top into a crisp layer of sugar. The green apple is mildly sour, and the sugar layer is like crème brûlée. Utterly delicious. The foie gras mousse melts in the mouth. Truly superb.

### Marinated Hamachi, Smoked Oysters, Cucumber and Pickles
image: ./images/lasarte/09.jpg | Marinated hamachi with smoked oysters and cucumber, Lasarte, Barcelona
Marinated hamachi with smoked oysters, cucumber and pickles. The fish is aged and not very fatty, with a sea aroma from the oysters and a green note from parsley. The acidity is open and bright, not sweet. It is a dish with a very good melody of flavours. The marine character carries over from the previous dish, but it lightens the heaviness and cleanses the palate.

### Fennel, Espardenyes and Iodized Sauce with Beluga Caviar
image: ./images/lasarte/10.jpg | Fennel with espardenyes and Beluga caviar, Lasarte, Barcelona
Fennel with espardenyes, the Spanish sea cucumber, in a sauce, topped with Beluga caviar. The crunchy Spanish sea cucumber contrasts with the cool, fragrant fennel. A dense, creamy block of fennel adds another dimension, and the light creamy sauce has a faint aroma of dill. The Beluga caviar lifts the sweetness of the sea cucumber and fennel with finesse, rather than being heaped on. Excellent.

### Clear Broth of Free-range Chicken and White Prawns, Barnacles and Aromatic Pineapple Emulsion
image: ./images/lasarte/11.jpg | Clear chicken and white prawn broth with goose barnacles, Lasarte, Barcelona
A clear broth of free-range chicken and white prawns, served with barnacles, or goose barnacles, a costly Spanish seafood ingredient, and an aromatic pineapple emulsion. In the bowl there are prawns, octopus tentacle, sea urchin, beetroot, mountain vegetables, verbena gel and onion. Complex, soft and delicious, not marine in aroma. The broth is salty, with a hint of chilli, and every spoonful is different. It is a dish that plays with the tongue and with flavour in a most interesting way.

### Squid Tartare with Green Apple Juice and Licorice
image: ./images/lasarte/12.jpg | Squid tartare with green apple juice and liquorice, Lasarte, Barcelona
Squid tartare in green apple juice, with black garlic and liquorice running through it. The sourness of the apple leads, the squid is sweet, and the garlic balances the flavour.

### Cured Scallop Slices and its Coral, Seabed and Vegetables
image: ./images/lasarte/13.jpg | Cured scallop slices with coral and vegetables, Lasarte, Barcelona
Thinly sliced cured scallop with scallop coral grilled until crisp, surrounded by a mung bean ice cream and vegetables formed into beads, a fish consommé jelly, and a sauce of mussels and herbs. You would not believe they go together, but eaten together it is delicious. The scallop is sweet, the ice cream cool and green, the flavour complex. Everything blends together in a puzzling way, and the textures are very interesting.

### Crustaceans Ravioli in its Own Essence, Burrata and Champagne
image: ./images/lasarte/14.jpg | Crustacean ravioli in its own essence with burrata and Champagne, Lasarte, Barcelona
Ravioli filled with shellfish in its own essence, served with burrata, the fresh Italian cheese with a creamy centre, grilled langoustine, Champagne foam and caviar. The grilled langoustine is superb, the Champagne foam lightly sour, the caviar large-grained. The salty ravioli adds another dimension, and the thick pasta holds in the juiciness and the aroma of many kinds of seafood very well.

### Duroc Pig's Trotters with Aniseed Herbs Pesto, Aubergine and Raifort
image: ./images/lasarte/15.jpg | Duroc pig's trotters with aniseed herb pesto and aubergine, Lasarte, Barcelona
Duroc pig's trotters, the pig breed from America known for the marbling of fat in its meat, smoked and cooked down to a gelatinous texture, served with aubergine, mushrooms, sliced black truffle, a jus, an aniseed-scented herb pesto, and a raifort cream, the French name for horseradish. The jus is umami and round, neither too strong nor too light. The sour pesto cuts the fat of the trotters, with a faint cool fragrance that helps blend the richness into the cream. The cream is intense, boosting flavour and reducing the greasiness. Each is delicious on its own and they balance one another, but eaten together they are better still.

### Marinated Venison Loin, Truffle Roots, Amarena Cream, Beetroot and Pink Pepper
image: ./images/lasarte/16.jpg | Marinated venison loin with amarena cream and beetroot, Lasarte, Barcelona
Venison loin marinated in black cherry, served with truffle roots, beetroot, pink pepper and amarena cream, the sour cherry cream of Italy. The venison is tender and not gamey, with the aroma of the open fire and a gentle saltiness. The jus is umami, and the cream is rich, though personally I think a little too much roux, the butter-and-flour thickener, went in. The beetroot leads with sourness and an earthy bitterness, with a crunchy texture.

DESSERTS

### Pistachio, Celery, Lime and Green Apple
image: ./images/lasarte/17.jpg | Pistachio, celery, lime and green apple, Lasarte, Barcelona
Celery ice cream with pistachio, lime and a green apple sphere. Unbelievably delicious, and the flavour of the green apple sphere follows through.

### Baba Spheres, Orange Blossom and Angostura
image: ./images/lasarte/18.jpg | Baba spheres with orange blossom and Angostura, Lasarte, Barcelona
Spheres inspired by the baba, the French rum-soaked cake, with orange blossom cream and Angostura, the herbal bitters used in cocktails. The spheres differ from one another: the orange blossom cream and the tangerine cream in the centre lift the orange liqueur aroma, while the dark sphere is another kind of orange in the Angostura style.

### Petit-fours

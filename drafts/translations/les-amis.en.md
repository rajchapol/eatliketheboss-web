Not long ago, the fine dining world was shaken when **L'Ambroisie**, the legendary institution of French cuisine in Paris that had held three Michelin stars for so long, was downgraded by the Michelin Guide to just two. As winds of change sweep through global fine dining, many legendary restaurants have had to adapt, turning towards innovation and novelty. Yet in Southeast Asia there is one institution that stands gracefully against the passage of time. "Les Amis" has not only held on firmly to its three Michelin stars since 2019; it is also one of my beloved "favourite restaurants", a place that holds beautiful memories with every visit.

Les Amis was founded in 1994 as one of the first standalone fine dining restaurants in Singapore. The name "Les Amis" means "friends" in French, reflecting its beginnings with a group of food lovers coming together. It is led by Executive Chef Sébastien Lepinoy, who worked with the legendary Joël Robuchon for 17 years. Chef Sébastien keeps to his French classic style and refuses to bring in Asian ingredients or turn to fusion, devoting himself wholly to the way of French Haute Cuisine, using only top-class ingredients, over 90% of them from artisanal suppliers in France, combined with precise, flawless technique. Holding on to its three stars is therefore not a matter of luck but of **consistency that comes through directly, which is the very essence of haute cuisine**.

The restaurant is decorated in luxurious, classic style, yet it offers the warmth its name promises. Every element is lifted to perfection by more than 3,000 labels of fine wine from a Wine Spectator Grand Award-level cellar, especially the superb collections from Burgundy and Bordeaux, along with flawless, seamless service. The sommeliers and staff are full of knowledge and explain things vividly while staying relaxed and friendly. Sipping a Château d'Yquem of twenty-odd years by the glass, from a 6-litre bottle, is truly not something you can do every day.

Les Amis is the "guardian" of haute French culinary art whose value time cannot diminish. In an age when the world chases novelty, this restaurant stands firm and proves that consistency and attention to detail are the heart of Gastronomy. A meal here is not merely about enjoying deliciousness but about immersing yourself in the aesthetics of perfect flavour. It is ideal for a special meal where you want to create precious memories, or for a group of gourmets longing for the essence of Classic French Cuisine amid the chaos of the world.

For me, returning to Les Amis is not just about enjoying a three-Michelin-star meal; it is about soaking up the impression it leaves and recalling precious memories.

## The art of taste in this meal

### Le pain au levain et son beurre extra fin de chez Beillevaire
image: ./images/les-amis/01.jpg | A large block of butter stamped with the restaurant's logo on a silver-rimmed plate, Les Amis, Singapore
We begin with something simple yet grand: sourdough bread served with Beillevaire artisanal butter from France, made by hand and ordered specially for the restaurant alone. The aroma of excellent butter combines with the crisp crust and soft crumb of the bread, a classic touch that awakens our expectations for the meal.

### Le Canape & L'amuse-bouche (Young Comté wrapped in viande des Grisons)
image: ./images/les-amis/02.jpg | Crossed sticks of Comté wrapped in red cured meat on a white ceramic stand, Les Amis, Singapore
The appetite is stirred with Comté cheese, delicately mild from the Jura mountains, wrapped in Viande des Grisons, a cured meat with a well-rounded saltiness. The lightness of the cheese contrasts with the deep umami of the meat, an ingenious way to open the senses.

### Morel velouté with foie gras royale & black truffle coulis
image: ./images/les-amis/03.jpg | Frothy velouté dotted with black truffle sauce in a white ceramic coffee cup, Les Amis, Singapore
A smooth, soft, warm soup with the rounded flavour of foie gras royale, fragrant with morel mushrooms and a delicate black truffle coulis. The richness of the cream is designed to be just right, never so heavy that it overwhelms the other flavours, an excellent balance.

### Le Caviar Osciètre (Poached egg in a fountain of watercress paired with spring turnip & Oscietra caviar) 🕶️
image: ./images/les-amis/04.jpg | Poached egg covered in fresh green watercress sauce beside Oscietra caviar on a silver-rimmed plate, Les Amis, Singapore
If there is one dish that plays the melody of flavour flawlessly, this is the answer. Premium Oscietra caviar from Maison Nordic in Paris gives a salty umami and a texture that bursts in the mouth, set against the gentle sweetness and distinctive aroma of spring turnip, while the watercress velouté brings a fresh green note, and herbs such as chervil act as a bridge that brings all the flavours to embrace the wonderfully tender poached egg. A complexity that melds into one, quite miraculously.

### Le Crustacé (Langoustine from Loctudy & emulsion of unfiltered extra virgin French olive oil sauce)
image: ./images/les-amis/05.jpg | A server pouring olive oil emulsion over langoustine wrapped in courgette and topped with caviar, Les Amis, Singapore
The first touch is the springy bite and deep sweetness of the langoustine, a top-quality shellfish sent straight from the port of Loctudy in Brittany, France. The freshness of the flesh is highlighted by an emulsion whisked from cold-pressed French olive oil, giving light, fragrant nutty notes without any green bitterness. A simple dish that truly reflects superior technique.

### Le Poisson De Mer (Wild turbot with baby green peas & vin jaune sauce)
image: ./images/les-amis/06.jpg | White turbot topped with baby peas and chives in a golden Vin Jaune sauce, Les Amis, Singapore
Wild turbot, firm yet delicately soft and free of greasiness, served with a Vin Jaune sauce, the renowned yellow wine of France's Jura region, which gives a deep aroma reminiscent of nuts and sherry. The sauce is well rounded and not overly creamy, balancing the crisp, vivid sweetness of the baby green peas.

### Le Plat Principal De Viande (Spring lamb rack from Aveyron with combination of bell peppers & white asparagus)
image: ./images/les-amis/07.jpg | Grilled lamb rack with sauce beside a row of red, green and yellow bell peppers on a white plate, Les Amis, Singapore
A tender, delicate lamb rack with no gamey smell, from Aveyron, the region known for quality lamb in France. It is lightly seasoned to preserve the sweetness of the meat, and served with a mild sauce that is rather rich, alongside bell peppers and white asparagus, which cut through it very well.

### Le Fromage Au Chariot Ou Au Plateau (Artisanal spring cheese selections)
image: ./images/les-amis/08.jpg | Four French cheeses arranged on a blue-rimmed plate, Les Amis, Singapore
The savoury courses close with seasonal cheeses from a carefully curated cheese trolley, reflecting the art of choosing cheese the truly French way.

### Le Pré-Dessert (Assortment of freshly churned ice creams)
image: ./images/les-amis/09.jpg | Two scoops of ice cream in an oval white ceramic bowl on lace paper, Les Amis, Singapore
Cleansing the palate before the main dessert with premium-quality homemade ice cream from the trolley.

### Le Dessert (Variations of gariguette strawberry in a crisp sugar sphere)
image: ./images/les-amis/10.jpg | A pink sugar sphere on red-orange strawberry sauce, Les Amis, Singapore
A dessert celebrating the French Gariguette strawberry, loved by chefs the world over for its heady fragrance and its well-judged sweet-tart taste. It is served in a delicate glazed sugar sphere that hides a meringue base, fresh strawberries and a strawberry compote sauce inside, giving a crisp texture alternating with juiciness in every mouthful.

### Les Petits Fours (Traditional baked pastries - Canelé & Palmier) & Les Chocolats Au Plateau (Selection of chocolate bonbons)
image: ./images/les-amis/11.jpg | Canelé and French pastries on a white ceramic stand, Les Amis, Singapore
Classic French pastries, including the canelé, crisp outside and soft within, and the palmier, finishing with chocolate bonbons filled with various flavours, a perfect close to the meal.
